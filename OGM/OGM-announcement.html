Hi there,

The Keele Postgraduate Association (KPA) invites you to join us for our upcoming Ordinary General Meeting. This is your opportunity to engage in meaningful discussions, propose motions, and actively contribute to shaping the future of our vibrant postgraduate community.

You can meet with fellow postgraduates, vote on important issues, and make your voice heard on topics that matter to you.

🍕 Great news! Pizza and drinks will be provided for all attendees joining us in person. It's a perfect opportunity to network with fellow postgraduates in a relaxed setting.

Submit a Motion

We invite all postgraduates to submit motions for discussion at the OGM. Your voice matters, and this is your chance to bring forward issues that are important to you and the postgraduate community.

Motion Submission Period

5th February - 15th February 2025

All motions must be submitted to kpa.chair@keele.ac.uk by the deadline.

Please find attached the KPA General Meeting Motions Template and an Example Motion to guide you in structuring your submission.
