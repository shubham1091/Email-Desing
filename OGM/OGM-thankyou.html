⚡ Action Required Your Voice Matters

Share Your Feedback on the KPA - SU Transition

As discussed at today's meeting, the University has confirmed that from 1 August 2026, they will move to funding a single student organisation combining the KPA and Keele SU.

This is a University decision but how this transition happens is something we can shape together. We are committed to protecting postgraduate identity, the Clubhouse, and your independent voice within any new model.

We want to hear from every postgraduate not just those who attended today. Please take 3-5 minutes to share your thoughts using the link below.

We're asking:

💬 What questions do you have about the KPA - SU transition?

🏛️ What matters most to you about protecting the KPA's identity?

🎯 What events and support do you want from KPA going forward?

💡 How can KPA better support your wellbeing, career and academic journey?

Share My Feedback →

Responses are anonymous unless you choose to include your name. All feedback will be reviewed by the KPA executive team and shared with the University as part of the consultation process.
