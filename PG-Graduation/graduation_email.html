LIFELONG MAGIC

Gordon Mousinho: A 50-Year Journey with Keele

1971: Edward Heath was PM, Starbucks was just starting, and a young Gordon Mousinho took his first steps onto Keele campus. He was the first in his family to go to university.

2025: 54 years later, Gordon (71) has just graduated with his sixth degree from Keele a PhD in Creative Writing!

"I always say Keele chose me rather than the other way around... To keep fit mentally, learning and education is the best thing you can do."

Gordon has completed a BA, PGCE, two MAs, and two PhDs at Keele. His latest work on the history of the US marks a full circle moment, assessed by the son of the very academics who taught him in the 70s.

A truly magical legacy. What will your story be?

A MARVELLOUS REUNION

Dr. Toby Jones & Neil 'Nello' Baldwin

Almost 11 years since the BBC’s touching drama Marvellous, BAFTA winner Toby Jones (who played Neil) returned to Keele to receive an honorary doctorate.

It was an emotional moment as he reunited with the legendary Neil 'Nello' Baldwin himself.

"Keele is such an extraordinary and unique place... Filming Marvellous here was one of the happiest shoots I've ever been involved in."

Toby, widely regarded as one of the UK's most versatile actors (Harry Potter, Indiana Jones, Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy), was honored for his extraordinary contribution to drama.
