🌸 Gallop into the Year of the Horse 🌸

Bold Steps, Bright Futures & Lunar Celebrations 🐴✨

Issue 9 | February 16, 2026

Dear amazing postgrads,

How are you truly doing? As the mist rolls over our 600-acre campus and we find ourselves in that unique North Staffordshire "between-seasons" weather, we wanted to take a moment to check in. Whether you're hunkered down in the library or grabbing a Costa at Chapters Café, know that the KPA is here for you.

This year is all about bold steps and chasing those research ambitions—it's the Year of the Horse, after all! From our spectacular Lunar New Year celebration at Keele Hall to city-wide festivities across the Potteries, we're embracing the spirit of decisive action and new beginnings.

We've also got some serious academic highlights to share, from Keele's £11 million mental health research grant to fascinating debates on the future of higher education. Plus, don't miss your chance to have your say at our upcoming Ordinary General Meeting—your voice truly matters.

So grab a warm drink, settle in, and let's explore everything happening this fortnight!

Stay awesome,
Shubham

THIS WEEK'S HIGHLIGHTS

Must-Attend Events

🏮 🏮 🏮

🐴 Lunar New Year Celebration: Year of the Horse

Sunday, February 16 | Keele Hall

"Gallop into the Year of the Horse with bold steps and bright futures!"

Welcome the Year of the Horse—a symbol of decisive action, ambition, and the pursuit of new challenges. Join us at the historic Keele Hall for an interactive and festive celebration featuring three courses of East Asian cuisine, professional Lion Dance performances where you can interact with life-size costumes, and creative craft activities.

What to Expect:

• 獅子舞 (Lion Dance) - Professional performance with audience interaction
• Three-course East Asian buffet with diverse flavors
• Craft activities and traditional music
• A warm, inclusive atmosphere—perfect if you're far from home!

Open to all students. Dress to impress and bring your appetite for celebration!

🗳️ Your Voice Matters: Ordinary General Meeting (OGM)

Tuesday, February 18 | 4:30-6:30 PM | DH1.13

"Your community, your voice. This is how we shape the future of KPA together."

The OGM is your opportunity to hear directly from President Anne George and Vice-President Sandra Suresh about what we've been working on in University Council and Faculty Education Committees. This isn't just bureaucracy—it's an interactive and fun way to influence the association's future.

On the Agenda:

• Officer reports on KPA activities and committee attendance
• Budget updates and funding allocations for student welfare
• Open forum for questions and policy proposals
• Networking with researchers across disciplines

Whether it's more Clubhouse space or better bus links—this is where we make it happen. Democracy in action!

🏮 Lantern Making Workshop

Friday, February 20 | 4:00-6:00 PM | KPA Clubhouse Upstairs

"Create your own piece of the Lunar New Year celebration!"

Extend the Year of the Horse celebrations with our hands-on lantern making workshop! Whether you're continuing the festivities from Sunday's celebration or discovering this tradition for the first time, join us for an afternoon of creativity and cultural exchange at the KPA Clubhouse.

What You'll Do:

• Learn traditional lantern-making techniques
• Create your own decorative lantern to take home
• All materials provided—just bring your creativity!
• Perfect for all skill levels, no experience needed

A relaxed, social workshop to brighten up your study space and connect with fellow postgrads. Open to all KPA members!

Weekly Rhythms

Academic Excellence

Keele is making waves! Check out these exciting research milestones and thought-provoking debates.

💰 £11 Million Mental Health Research Grant

Keele's Mental Health Research Group (MHRG) has secured £11 million in NIHR funding to transform regional mental health support. For postgrads in psychology, sociology, and medical sciences, this creates fertile ground for applied research with direct local impact—proof that we can achieve global research prestige while serving our immediate community.

🌍 The Global University Debate

Monday, February 17 | Westminster Theatre, Chancellor's Building

"Are we exporting education or exploiting students?"
Particularly relevant for international postgrads, this session examines the sustainability of the current UK higher education model in a globalized market.

🎯 Local Lives vs. Global Goals

Tuesday, February 25 | Denise Coates Foundation Building

Should regional universities like Keele prioritize local community improvement or global research ambitions? A framework for considering the "impact" agenda of your work.

🎓 Success Stories: Leverhulme Master's Plus Graduates

Congratulations to Grace Buffery and Haadhi Shabar, who successfully transitioned from the Leverhulme Master's Plus program into PhD studies! Their journey demonstrates Keele's commitment to improving representation in academia and serves as inspiration for current postgrads navigating their own academic career paths.
