Checking in with you.

Dear students,

We understand that recent major events unfolding globally and within the UK are causing profound distress, uncertainty, and concern for many. It is incredibly difficult to focus on academic life when the news cycle is relentlessly heavy and communities are hurting.

If you are feeling overwhelmed, distracted, or anxious during this time, please know that your feelings are entirely valid.

You do not have to navigate this alone. The KPA and the wider university have robust support systems in place designed to hold you up during moments exactly like this. We strongly encourage you to take advantage of these resources if you need them.

Your Immediate Support Network

1 Spectrum.Life Assistance
Available 24/7. Freephone 0800 031 8227 or text "Hi" to 07418 260 780 for immediate emotional support.
2 Academic Flexibility (ECs)
If you cannot study, apply for Exceptional Circumstances (ECs) on eVision. These exist exactly for situations outside your control.
3 The Clubhouse Drop-In
Sometimes you just need a cup of tea and a safe space offline. The KPA Clubhouse is always open for you.

We are a community, and our first priority is taking care of each other. Please step away from your screens if you need to, hold your loved ones close, and reach out to us if you need help navigating your university commitments.
