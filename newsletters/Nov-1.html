🗓️ November Calendar

🧠 & 💼 November Focus Wellbeing & Career Support

📚 Weekly Study Sessions (Every Monday)
Quiet, friendly space to study together in the KPA Clubhouse. Bring your notes, plug in your laptop, and work alongside other postgrads for a focused, low-pressure session. We'll have free hot drinks and 50% off on food perfect for keeping energy up while you tackle essays or lab work. Drop in any Monday; no sign-up needed.
When: Every Monday
Time: 11:00 am - 04:30 pm
Venue: KPA Clubhouse
💖 Mental Health Workshop (Self-Love)
Join us for a relaxed, guided online session focused on self-care, balance, and small practical techniques to manage stress. The workshop blends short breathing and grounding exercises with easy-to-use tips you can apply between meetings and experiments. It's open to everyone and designed especially for the pressures of postgraduate study bring a hot drink and a comfy chair.
Date: Mon 17 Nov
Time: 6:30 pm
Venue: Online (Teams)
💼 PG Careers Drop-In
Drop in for friendly, practical advice from the Careers team whether you want CV or application feedback, interview practice, or pointers on next steps after your degree. These sessions are relaxed and informal: bring a CV, application draft, or a short project summary and you'll get a focused 1:1 conversation with actionable tips.

Our advisers can help you tailor your application materials for academic and non-academic roles, suggest sensible next steps, and signpost useful resources and training. No appointment is needed just pop by during the drop-in slots below.
Date: Thu 13 Nov & Thu 27 Nov
Time: 10:30 am - 12:30 pm
Venue: KPA Clubhouse
