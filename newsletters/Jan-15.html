Exceptional Circumstances (EC) Policy

Two types of support available for unforeseen circumstances affecting your assessment performance:
Source: Regulation B3: Exceptional Circumstances

Option 1: 7-Day Automatic Extension

7

CALENDAR DAYS

Extension period

3

MAXIMUM USES

Per semester

Who can use: PGT students (taught modules)
No evidence required for these extensions
Must apply BEFORE original deadline
Does not apply to: Exams, class tests, presentations
Exception: Students with disability adjustments = unlimited extensions (1 per assessment)

Option 2: Another Assessment Opportunity (AAO)

For significant, unforeseen issues requiring more than 7 days:
• Complete new work at subsequent assessment period
• Requires supporting evidence
• Must submit within 7 calendar days of original deadline
• Assessed case-by-case

Apply through eVision System

Emergency on exam day? Contact your School office AND support.dsi@keele.ac.uk immediately.
Full policy: EC Code of Practice

Critical: Late Submission Penalties (Regulation D1)

Unauthorized late submission has severe consequences:

Within 7 Days

Work is marked but grade capped at 50% (pass mark). Even if work deserves 75%, recorded mark = 50%.

Missing by 1 hour = cap applied

Beyond 7 Days

Work not accepted. Recorded mark = 0% (Fail). Requires reassessment.

Missing by 8 days = zero mark

Use your 7-day automatic extension BEFORE the deadline if you need more time. Waiting until after deadline = penalty applied.

"Fit to Sit" Policy

By submitting an assessment or entering an exam hall, you declare yourself fit to be assessed.

• Extremely difficult to claim EC after submission
• Retrospective claims only if you were incapable of determining fitness (e.g., emergency hospitalization)
• If unwell: do NOT submit/sit — claim EC instead

Compensation (Condonement) Rules (Regulation D2)

Marginal fails (40-49%) can be condoned without resit:

✓ Module mark between 40-49% (below 40% = "bad fail", not compensable)
✓ Maximum 30 credits compensated across entire degree
✓ Weighted average ≥ 55% across all modules
✗ Dissertation/Project module cannot be compensated (must pass)

Academic Misconduct: Penalty Tariff (Regulation D4)

Minor Offense (First Time)

Small plagiarism, poor citation, minor AI misuse
Penalty: Formal warning, may resubmit with mark capped at 50%

Major Offense / Second Offense

Significant plagiarism, repeat offense, exam misconduct, major AI misuse
Penalty: 0% for entire MODULE (not just assignment). If reassessment permitted, capped at 50%.

Grave Offense

Contract cheating (essay mills), data falsification, third plagiarism offense
Penalty: Termination of studies. Withdrawn with no award.

All work screened via Turnitin. Cite AI usage (ChatGPT, etc.) as you would any source. Check assessment brief for AI permissions.
Read more: Generative AI in Education Framework

Borderline Classification: The Dissertation Advantage (Regulation D2)

Your dissertation can promote you to the next classification if you're on the borderline:

Distinction Promotion

Overall average: 68-69%
Dissertation: ≥70%
→ Awarded Distinction

Merit Promotion

Overall average: 58-59%
Dissertation: ≥60%
→ Awarded Merit

The dissertation is the "kingmaker" module. Strategic priority for borderline students.

Feedback Guarantee: Expect feedback within 15 working days of submission deadline (excludes weekends, Bank Holidays, Christmas). Dissertations may take longer (communicated in advance).
Source: Assessment and Feedback Code of Practice
