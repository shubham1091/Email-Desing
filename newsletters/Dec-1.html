Welcome to December: Celebrating & Supporting You Through the Winter Break

Hello postgraduates,

Welcome to December. We have officially reached the end of the first semester, and whether you are submitting final assignments, working in the labs, or preparing to travel home, you should be incredibly proud of what you have achieved this term.

As the festive lights illuminate Union Square and the winter chill sets in, the rhythm of campus life is changing. We know this time of year brings a mix of emotions—excitement for the holidays, but perhaps also anxiety about deadlines or feelings of isolation if you are staying in Staffordshire.

This newsletter is your handbook for the next four weeks. We have curated a list of the best festive events in Keele, Newcastle, and Stoke to keep your calendar full. More importantly, we have compiled a comprehensive guide to Winter Support—detailing exactly which services are open, how to access healthcare, and where to find community during the closure period.

The KPA Clubhouse remains open for most of the break, including our special Christmas Day Brunch. You are part of a strong, resilient community, and we are here to support you.

❄️ Winter Break: Service Closures & Hours (Essential Info)

The period Dec 20 – Jan 5 is critical. Plan ahead now.

📚 Library Access

  • 24/7 access ENDS: Friday, December 19
  • Reduced hours Dec 20-23 & Dec 29-30: 9am–5pm (check website for exact times)
  • CLOSED: Dec 25, 26 & Jan 1
  • Action: Download all digital resources and borrow physical books by Dec 19

🏢 Student Services Hub

  • CLOSES: 5:00 PM, Tuesday, December 23
  • REOPENS: 9:00 AM, Monday, January 5
  • Action: Submit visa advice, funding requests, or official letter requests by Dec 15 to ensure processing

🚌 Bus Service (First Potteries Route 25)

  • Dec 24 (Christmas Eve): Saturday service, finishing early (6–8pm)
  • Dec 25 & 26 (Christmas/Boxing Day): NO SERVICE – Campus is isolated
  • Dec 27–30: Saturday service
  • Dec 31 (New Year's Eve): Saturday service, finishing early
  • Jan 1 (New Year's Day): NO SERVICE
  • Action: Complete all grocery shopping by Dec 24. Taxis charge premium rates on bank holidays.

🏪 Shops & Food Access

  • Campus Co-op & supermarkets: CLOSED Christmas Day
  • Action: Stock up on essentials by Dec 23

🚨 Emergency & Mental Health Support (24/7)

  • Campus Safety (On-Campus Emergencies): 01782 733999
  • Spectrum.Life (Mental Health): 24/7 confidential counseling via phone or app
  • NHS Walk-in Center: Haywood Hospital, Burslem
  • A&E (Life-Threatening Only): Royal Stoke University Hospital

🎪 Beyond Campus: Newcastle, Stoke & Trentham

Newcastle-under-Lyme

  • Lantern Parade (Dec 6, 4:30 PM): FREE community procession with handmade lanterns. Visually spectacular.
  • New Vic Theatre — "The Little Mermaid" (Nov 14 – Jan 24): Unique in-the-round production with circus elements. Premium cultural experience.
  • Castle Artisan Market (Dec 13 & 21): Winter Food & Drink Festival with high-quality local produce.

Stoke-on-Trent (Hanley)

  • Winter Wonderland Stoke (Nov 21 – Jan 4): Opposite Hanley Bus Station. Real ice rink, Bavarian bar, rides. FREE entry, paid attractions. Special: Taylor Swift Tribute (Dec 13).
  • Regent Theatre — "Cinderella" Pantomime (from Dec 12): Starring Jonathan Wilkes. High-energy, audience participation. Quintessential British cultural experience for international students.

Trentham Estate

  • Winter Light Trail (Nov 21 – Jan 4): 1-mile illuminated walk through historic gardens. Premium experience (£17+ ticket). Visually stunning.
  • Christmas Makers Markets (Dec 6–7): FREE to access. High-end shopping experience.

💡 Tip: Prioritize FREE events (Lantern Parade, Chapel Concerts, Postgrad Open) and paid events as "treats" rather than expectations. Economic sensitivity matters during winter break.

🌍 International Students: Visa & Work Rights

Full-time work (20+ hours) is permitted:

  • PGT (Masters): Winter vacation (Dec 20 – Jan 11) is an official vacation period. Full-time work allowed.
  • PGR (PhD/Research): No standard vacation terms. Continue adhering to 20-hour limit unless you've requested authorized annual leave. Breaching this risks visa issues.
