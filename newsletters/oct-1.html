Hello postgraduates,

We hope you’re all settling in after a busy Welcome Week! Whether this is your very first semester at Keele or you’re returning to continue your postgraduate journey, the KPA is here to make sure you feel at home. New beginnings can be exciting but also overwhelming, so remember — you’re not alone. If you ever need support, advice, or just a friendly chat, we’re only an email or a Clubhouse visit away.

This Welcome Week was filled with energy, laughter, and plenty of new connections. From meet-and-greets and workshops to socials at the Clubhouse, it was fantastic to see so many of you come together. The photo collage above captures just a few of those memorable moments a reminder that our postgraduate community is vibrant, supportive, and always growing.

Honestly, the whole week felt like it was straight out of a playlist — pure “2 Days Into College” vibe 🎵 — full of excitement, curiosity, and the joy of starting something new.

☎️ Keele Telethons — Become a Student Caller

Every year, Keele students connect with alumni through our Telethon campaign. It’s a chance to build relationships, share what’s happening at Keele, and raise funds that directly support current and future students. Flexible, paid shifts make this a great way to gain valuable experience while helping the community.

What to expect

  • Remote calling sessions (alumni are notified in advance)
  • Training and support provided; calling app supplied
  • On average, 40–50% of alumni donate or reconnect
  • Develop confidence, communication, and negotiation skills
