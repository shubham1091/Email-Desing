Welcome back to the grind, postgrads,

The beginning of May marks a critical transition. We are moving out of the instructional phases of the spring term and directly into the rigours of assessment, examination, and independent thesis-writing. We know this phase can feel deeply isolating and undeniably intense.

We see the atmospheric anxieties you are carrying. Whether you are navigating Level 7 assessments, structuring endless literature reviews, or feeling the heavy silence of the library, your stress is entirely valid. But you don't have to navigate this alone.

This edition is your comprehensive academic toolkit for the weeks ahead. It includes the absolute, uncompromising details on election cut-offs, assessment safety nets, and crucial administrative deadlines. But it also features a robust line-up of community events and regional festivals designed to get you out of the academic bubble.

Read carefully, note your deadlines, and remember to protect your peace. We've got your back. Let's finish this term strong. 💪

Stay bold,
Shubham

The Spring Academic Toolkit

Navigating Spring Assessments, Deadlines, & Admin.

Navigating May Deadlines 🍂

For us postgrads, May is defined by two crucial paths:

1. The Realities of Placement Season

For January intake students aiming for a 6-to-12-month industry placement, you must request this transfer during May. Remember: it's highly competitive! You need an average of at least 50% across Semester 1. International students must consult with their Programme Director and the ICA team immediately to navigate visa modifications according to UKVI rules.

2. Reassessment Season Preparation

From May 11 to May 22, we enter a dedicated preparation window for resits and resubmissions. Academic trajectories are rarely linear. See this period as an opportunity for mastery and academic redemption.

Level 7 Assessments & Safety Nets 🛟

For our taught postgrads, the formal assessment window runs from Monday, 18 May to Friday, 29 May. This requires immense cognitive stamina.

Exceptional Circumstances (ECs)

You have an automatic 7-day extension for up to three pieces of written coursework per semester—no evidentiary documentation required. Apply via eVision BEFORE the deadline expires!

The Dual-Dropbox System

Remember the KLE digital architecture: The primary dropbox locks exactly at the deadline. If you use a 7-day extension or submit late, you must use the secondary dropbox, which only appears after the original deadline passes. Don't panic when the first one disappears!

Welfare & The Writer's Block 🖋️

Thesis writing is profoundly isolating. But you don't have to stay in your silo.

  • Writing Retreat for Researchers (May 27, 14:00 - 16:00): Hosted by Library Research Services and the KDA, this structured hybrid session offers focused writing sprints and mandatory breaks. 15 in-person seats (Campus Library) and 50 online seats.
  • Digital Support: Tap into the "In a Nutshell" modules or "Study Matters Podcast" for late-night guidance on referencing or conquering maths anxiety.
  • Service Disruption Notice: The Student Services Hub (Claus Moser) is CLOSED tomorrow (Bank Holiday Monday, May 4). If you hit acute burnout this bank holiday weekend, use the 24/7 Spectrum Life helpline (0800 031 8227). It is our primary emergency brake.

Escape the Bubble 🌳

Leave the 600-acre campus and explore Staffordshire.

  • The Staffy Trail (Throughout May): Celebrate the 10th Anniversary of Staffordshire Day with this free, app-guided heritage walking trail featuring giant Staffordshire Bull Terrier sculptures across Stoke-on-Trent, Tamworth, and Stafford.
  • Pint of Science (May 18-19): Catch informal scientific lectures at Mellards in Newcastle-under-Lyme. Bridge academia and social relaxation in a pub setting!
  • Lymelight Festival (May 23-24): Head to Newcastle-under-Lyme for a massive free-entry grassroots music festival. Check out the "6 Towns Radio Acoustic Stage" sponsored by Keele outside 'Keele in Town'.
  • The Great British Food Festival (Early May): Experience artisan stalls and award-winning street food at the stunning Trentham Estate.
