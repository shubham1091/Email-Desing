The 190m Advantage

The "Keele Microclimate"

Did you know Keele Campus sits 190 meters above sea level? That's significantly higher than Stoke or Newcastle town centres.

What this means for you: While it might be raining in Hanley, that extra elevation drops the temperature just enough to turn rain into sleet or snow up here. We are in the "Goldilocks Zone" for winter weather!

Winter Break Essentials

Before you head home or settle in for the break, sort these practical things so you can relax without worry.

☀️ Prioritize Daylight
Get outside at noon, sit near windows, or use a SAD lamp for 20 minutes to beat the winter blues.
🚶 Low-Stress Movement
Boost your mood with a short walk or light stretching—no intense workouts required.
🕰️ Stick to a Rhythm
Keep your sleep and meal times consistent to maintain energy levels.
🤝 Connect on purpose
Schedule one small social plan each week — a call, a coffee or a shared walk — even when you don't feel like it.
🛋️ Soften Your Space
Use warm lighting, blankets, and a tidy corner to instantly transform your room's vibe.
🧣 Stay Warm
Layer up, keep your living area around 18°C, and use hot water bottles to stay comfortable.
