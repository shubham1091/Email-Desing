Crushing Assignments & Protecting Your Peace ✨

Issue 10 | March 3, 2026

Hey there amazing postgrads,

March is always a chaotic month, but this year it feels fundamentally different. The news cycle is relentlessly heavy. Between the ongoing global crises, escalating geopolitical conflicts, and distressing humanitarian emergencies, trying to focus on academic research can feel completely surreal and sometimes impossible.

We see you, and we see how profoundly this is affecting our community's mental health. It is entirely valid to feel overwhelmed, distracted, or exhausted right now.

In the midst of all this, university life marches on, and deadlines are still looming. Surviving this crunch doesn't just mean drinking more coffee it means finding your rhythm, building protective safety nets, and knowing exactly when to clock out and protect your peace.

In this highly focused edition, we are breaking down the Ultimate Assignment Playbook designed for high-stress times: the Before, the During, and the glorious After. We've also loaded this up with our favorite KPA social events, because connection with each other is the ultimate antidote to despair. Let's support each other and conquer this month. �

Stay bold,
Shubham

The Assignment Playbook

Your step-by-step masterclass to keeping your sanity intact.

Phase 1

BEFORE: The Storm 🌩️

Never just "start writing." When the prompt drops, your very first move is preparation.

The Reality Check: Exceptional Circumstances

If global events are severely impacting your capacity to study, apply for Exceptional Circumstances (ECs) immediately via your eVision portal. The university has frameworks to support you do not wait until the night before a deadline if you are struggling now. Expecting a sudden crisis? Note that Keele uses a 28-Hour Window for many open-book/digital assessments to provide flexibility.

🎯 Define The Inquiry

Once you have the mental space to begin, break down your prompt immediately using the FINERMAPS criteria (is it Feasible, Novel, Ethical?). Knowing exactly what you are writing about saves 20 hours of lost research.

Phase 2

DURING: The Grind 😤

You are in the deepest trenches of the literature review. It's tough, but you can navigate the noise out here.

Employ Citation Chaining (forward/backward snowballing) so you don't get lost in infinite tabs. Build a Synthesis Matrix to instantly spot gaps in your argument!

⚠️ EMERGENCY BRAKES

If your brain stalls, force a disconnect. Take the 'Secret Cut-Through' from Clarice Cliff Court to Aldi for fresh air. Hitting genuine burnout? Pick up your phone and dial the 24/7 Spectrum.Life helpline (0800 031 8227). Talking it out is the ultimate hack.

Phase 3

AFTER: The Click 🎉

"Your submission has been received."

Turn off your laptop immediately. Do not under any circumstances re-read your essay you will only find a typo and ruin your week. Instead, dive straight into decompression mode.
