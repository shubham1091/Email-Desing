🗓️ Events remaining this month

Below are short descriptions so you know what to expect — most events do not require sign-ups, just turn up and enjoy. (Only Face Painting and the PG Drop-In require sign-up.)

Oktoberfest — 16–19 Oct
A cosy Clubhouse take on Oktoberfest: expect bratwurst, pretzels and seasonal specials. A relaxed social atmosphere — great for grabbing a bite and meeting other postgraduates. No booking required.
Diversity Fest — 20 Oct
Celebrate Keele’s many cultures with live music, dance, food stalls and society stands. Bring friends, try new foods, and enjoy performances throughout the day. No sign-up needed — just come along and join the fun.

📚 Keele Doctoral Academy — Research Development Series (13–24 Oct)

The Keele Doctoral Academy’s fortnight of workshops supports postgraduate researchers at every stage — whether you're refining methods, preparing publications, planning impact, or developing public engagement. Sessions are short, practical and designed to be applied immediately to your research.

Who should attend? Postgraduate researchers from all faculties, supervisors looking for teaching & supervision ideas, and research staff. There are hybrid sessions so you can join online or in person.

Example sessions this week include:

  • Research Impact & Engagement — turning ideas into public outputs
  • Writing for Publication — structure, peer review & responding to reviewers
  • Data Management & Open Research — practical steps for reproducibility
  • Career Pathways — academic and non-academic next steps

Tip: check the events page for session times and hybrid links — some sessions require pre-registration due to limited capacity.
