Issue 8 | February 1, 2026

Dear amazing postgrads,

Welcome to February—the month of love! And I'm not just talking about romantic love (though that's lovely too). I'm talking about community love, self-love, and the love of learning that brought you here in the first place.

This month, we're celebrating all kinds of connection. Whether that's connecting with new friends at our Valentine's bash (platonic pals very welcome!), connecting with your creative side at our Lantern Making night, or connecting with your community voice at our OGM meeting.

We've also got some serious support for your studies, from AI ethics workshops to our weekly study sessions where the coffee is always on.

Check out the 'Achievement Unlocked' game below—I want to see how many of you can get full marks this month!

Stay awesome,
Shubham

THIS MONTH'S HIGHLIGHTS

Must-Attend KPA Events

Valentine's Celebration

Friday, February 13 | KPA Clubhouse

"For the love of... community, connection, and really good snacks."

February is the month of love, and we're celebrating ALL forms of it! Whether you're celebrating with your partner, your best mates, your dissertation supervisor (Stockholm syndrome is real 😅), or treating yourself (self-love is the best kind of love!), everyone is welcome at our Valentine's bash.

What to Expect:

• Delicious treats and refreshments
• Board games and social activities
• A warm, welcoming atmosphere (no couple pressure!)
• The perfect study break to recharge

Bring yourself, bring a friend, or bring your dissertation—we'll help you celebrate any kind of love this February.

Register Now →

Ordinary General Meeting (OGM)

Wednesday, February 18 | KPA Clubhouse

"Your community, your voice. This is how we shape the future of KPA together."

The OGM is your opportunity to hear directly from the KPA team about what we're working on, what's coming up, and how we're using resources to support YOU.

On the Agenda:

• Updates on KPA initiatives and projects
• Budget and funding allocations
• Upcoming events and collaborations
• Open forum for questions and suggestions

This is democracy in action. Come along, have your say, and help us build a stronger postgraduate community.

Lantern Making Workshop

Friday, February 20 | KPA Clubhouse

"Beat the dark February evenings by making your own light. Chill vibes only."

There's something therapeutic about creating with your hands. Join us for a peaceful evening of lantern making—perfect for those who need a break from screens and deadlines.

What You'll Do:

• Craft your own beautiful paper lantern
• Decorate and personalize your design
• Take your creation home to brighten your space
• All materials provided—just bring yourself!

No artistic skills required. This is about relaxation, creativity, and making something beautiful to take home.

Academic Excellence

Keele Library is offering fantastic workshops this month to sharpen your research and writing skills

📚 The 100Book Club - Robin Ince LAUNCH

Sunday, February 9 | 6:00pm - 7:30pm | Chapter's Cafe

Join best-selling author and bibliomaniac Robin Ince for the 100Book Club launch. He'll be in conversation with Lisa Blower, reading from his new work "Normally Weird and Weirdly Normal", as the National Year of Reading begins.

📍 Free parking after 5pm | Fully accessible | Free entry, no booking required

Learn More

🔍 How the Library Can Support Your Research

Monday, February 10, 10:00am-11:00am | Hybrid (Campus Library + Microsoft Teams)

Essential for PG researchers! Discover the full range of library resources, expert guidance, and support services available to you. Learn about advanced search techniques, interlibrary loans, subject librarians, and specialized research tools.

Limited in-person seats—register early!

Register Now

🤖 AI Ethics and Research

Monday, February 17, 10:00am-11:00am | Online

Navigate the ethical landscape of AI in academia. This workshop covers how to use AI tools responsibly in your research, identifying ethical considerations, recognizing risks of bias and misinformation, and applying best practices for transparency and attribution.

A must-attend in 2026—understand the boundaries before you cross them!
