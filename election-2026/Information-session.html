KPA GENERAL ELECTION 2026  ·  INFORMATION SESSION

Thinking of Standing?
Come to Our Info Session

Tuesday 22 April 2026 at 5:30 PM  |  CBA 0.007 & MS Teams

Dear Postgraduate Community,

We are holding an open Information Session for anyone who is curious about standing in the 2026 KPA General Election. Whether you are seriously considering it or just want to understand what the roles involve, you are very welcome to join.

There is no commitment required, it is simply a chance to ask questions and find out more.

Event Details
Date:    Tuesday, 22 April 2026
Time:    5:30 PM to 7:00 PM
Location: CBA 0.007 & MS Teams
Open to: All registered postgraduate students

What we will cover

  • What each of the three roles involves on a day-to-day basis
  • How the nomination and campaign process works
  • The timeline from nomination to results
  • Background on the KPA and what it does for postgraduates
  • Open Q&A, bring any questions you have
  If you cannot attend, a recording will be made available on the KPA website afterwards. Nominations remain open until 4 May 2026.
