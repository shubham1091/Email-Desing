This Is Your Chance
To Lead the KPA.
Nominations Are Open Now.

Nominations for the KPA General Election 2026 are open right now, and we want to hear from you. We currently have very few nominees — which means this is a real and open opportunity for any postgraduate who has ever thought about making a difference at Keele. That could be you.

Dear Postgraduate Community,

We know postgraduate life is busy lab deadlines, thesis chapters, teaching hours. But here is the thing: the people who run the KPA next year will be the ones deciding what support you get, what events happen, and how your concerns are heard by the University.

You do not need a political background or prior experience. You just need a genuine desire to make postgraduate life at Keele better. If that sounds like you even a little please consider putting your name forward.

Why Nominate Yourself?
★ Shape your community. Directly influence events, welfare support, and how postgrad concerns reach the University.
★ Build real leadership skills. CV-worthy experience in advocacy, governance, and team management.
★ Full support provided. You won't be thrown in at the deep end outgoing officers will brief and support you every step of the way.
★ It is your year. This is one of the few chances to put your mark on Keele's postgraduate community before you graduate.
Three Positions Available
KPA President
Represents all postgraduate students at university-level committees and leads the KPA team. A high-impact role for someone who wants to advocate at the highest level.
Vice-President
Focuses on community events, student welfare, and the day-to-day experience of postgraduates at Keele. Great for someone who enjoys bringing people together.
Secretary (Part-time)
Manages communications and keeps the association running smoothly. A flexible, part-time commitment that suits postgraduates with busy research schedules.
