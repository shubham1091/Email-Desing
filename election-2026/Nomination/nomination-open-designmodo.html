Nominations open: 20 April 2026 , Deadline: 4 May 2026

Dear Postgraduate Community,

We are looking for motivated postgraduate students to stand for election and take on a leadership role within the Keele Postgraduate Association. Three positions are available for the 2026–27 academic year, and no prior experience in student representation is needed, just a genuine interest in supporting your fellow postgraduates.

If you have ever thought about getting more involved, this is a great opportunity to make a real difference to postgraduate life at Keele.

Available Positions
KPA President
Represents all postgraduate students at university-level committees and leads the KPA team. A high-impact role for someone who wants to advocate at the highest level.
Vice-President
Focuses on community events, student welfare, and the day-to-day experience of postgraduates at Keele. Great for someone who enjoys bringing people together.
Secretary (Part-time)
Manages communications and keeps the association running smoothly. A flexible, part-time commitment that suits postgraduates with busy research schedules.
