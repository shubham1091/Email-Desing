Your role at a glance

As Equality & Diversity Officer, you'll work to ensure that KPA activities are inclusive and accessible for all. Your key responsibilities include:

  • Promoting equality, diversity and inclusion across the postgraduate community.
  • Running at least one equality-focused campaign per year.
  • Ensuring accessibility is considered in all events and initiatives.
  • Representing postgraduate voices in University equality discussions.

You'll also collaborate with other officers, contribute to committees, and help shape Keele's postgraduate environment to be welcoming for everyone.
