Have You Put Your Name Forward Yet?

We sent an email on Monday and we want to follow up, because we still have very few nominees for the 2026 KPA election. That means three of the most important leadership roles in the Keele postgraduate community could go uncontested — or unfilled. We really do not want that to happen.

Dear Postgraduate Community,

We genuinely believe there is someone reading this email right now who would be a great KPA officer — but has talked themselves out of it. Too busy. Not experienced enough. Not sure it is for them.

We want to push back on that. The nomination form takes less than five minutes to fill in. You do not need experience in student politics. You just need to care about your postgraduate community and be willing to show up. That is genuinely enough to start.

Still Not Sure? Here Is the Reality
→ You will not be doing this alone — outgoing officers provide a full handover and ongoing support throughout the year.
→ The Secretary role is part-time — specifically designed to work alongside research and teaching commitments.
→ Nominating is not committing — if you get to the hustings and decide it is not right for you, that is okay.
→ Have a question first? Email kpa.elections@keele.ac.uk and we will get back to you same day.
