Keele Postgraduate Association • By-Elections (Oct–Nov 2025)
Join the Candidate Hustings — Monday 27 October, 5:00 PM
Hear from your postgraduate candidates for International Officer and Activities Officer, ask questions, and enjoy free pizza and soft drinks.

Dear Keele Postgraduate,

The Keele Postgraduate Association By-Election Candidate Hustings will take place on Monday, 27 October 2025. This event is your opportunity to meet the candidates standing to represent the postgraduate community, hear their plans and ideas, and take part in the discussion before voting opens.

📋 Event Details

📅 Date: Monday 27 October 2025
🕔 Time: 5:00 PM (doors open 4:45 PM)
📍 Location: CBA1.102 (Chancellor’s Building)
💻 Online Option: Microsoft Teams (link below)
🍕 Refreshments: Free pizza and soft drinks after the event

What will happen at Hustings?
  • Each candidate will give a 2-minute speech about their ideas, priorities, and motivations for running.
  • Audience members can then ask questions — one for each position — to help understand the candidates’ perspectives.
  • The event will be moderated by the Returning Officers to ensure fairness and respectful discussion.
  • A recording of the Hustings will be made available afterwards for those who cannot attend.

This is your chance to get to know your postgraduate representatives before casting your vote. Your engagement matters — every vote and every question helps shape the KPA leadership team.
