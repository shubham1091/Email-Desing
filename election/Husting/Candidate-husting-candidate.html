Keele Postgraduate Association • By-Elections (Oct–Nov 2025)
Candidate Hustings — Monday 27 October, 5:00 PM
This is a mandatory in-person event for all candidates — see attached agenda for timings and speaking order.

Dear Candidate,

The KPA By-Election Hustings will be held on Monday, 27 October 2025 at 5:00 PM in Room CBA1.102 (Chancellor’s Building). This is your opportunity to present your campaign to postgraduate members and respond to their questions before voting opens.

Attendance is mandatory. As outlined in Section 2.2 of the KPA By-Election Handbook, candidates must attend Hustings in person unless they have provided a valid reason for non-attendance at least 12 hours in advance. Failure to attend without prior notice will result in disqualification.

📋 Event Details

📅 Date: Monday 27 October 2025
🕔 Time: 5:00 PM (arrival and check-in by 4:45 PM)
📍 Venue: CBA1.102, Chancellor’s Building (in person only)

During Hustings
  • You will have 2 minutes to deliver your prepared speech.
  • You will then answer questions from members — up to 1 minute per question.
  • All candidates for the same role will be asked the same set of questions in turn.
  • A timekeeper and moderator will ensure fairness and equal speaking opportunities.

After the Hustings, all candidates are invited to join the informal networking session with members over pizza and refreshments.

Please review the attached KPA Candidate Hustings Agenda (PDF) for your exact speaking time and role order. You are expected to be present for the full duration of the event, not just your own segment.

If you cannot attend due to an exceptional reason, contact the Returning Officers immediately via kpa.elections@keele.ac.uk.
