Dear Postgraduates,

The Keele Postgraduate Association (KPA) By-Elections are now open. If you've ever wanted to make a difference, gain leadership experience, or run events and campaigns for postgraduates — now's your chance.

International Officer
Champion international students, run cultural initiatives and support transition activities.
