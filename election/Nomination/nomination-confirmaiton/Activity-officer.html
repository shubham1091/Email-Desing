Your role at a glance

As Activities Officer, you'll plan and deliver events that make postgraduate life vibrant and engaging. Your key responsibilities include:

  • Designing and organising activities, socials and cultural events for Keele postgraduates.
  • Collaborating with other KPA officers and Keele SU to ensure events are inclusive.
  • Providing opportunities for postgraduate students to connect and build community.
  • Ensuring events reflect the diverse interests and backgrounds of Keele PGs.

You'll play a central role in fostering community spirit and making Keele a lively, welcoming space for all postgraduates.
