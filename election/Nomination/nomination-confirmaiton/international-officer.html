🌍 Your role at a glance
As International Officer, you’ll ensure that the voices of international postgraduates are heard and supported. Your key responsibilities include:
  • Championing international students within the KPA and University discussions.
  • Running cultural initiatives and events that celebrate diversity.
  • Helping international students transition into life at Keele.
  • Leading at least one campaign on cultural understanding and internationalism.
You’ll also have opportunities to sit on University committees, collaborate with other officers, and contribute to shaping the postgraduate experience for our global community.
