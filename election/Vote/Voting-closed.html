Dear Keele Postgraduate,

Voting for the KPA By-Election has now closed at 5:00 pm. Thank you to everyone who participated. The Returning Officer team will now begin the count.

What happens next
  • Counting will take place immediately after the close of polls.
  • Results will be announced at 7:00 pm at the KPA Clubhouse and streamed online.
  • A full results email and breakdown will be sent to all members after the announcement.

If you experienced a technical issue while voting and believe your vote was not recorded, email kpa.elections@keele.ac.uk immediately with details and screenshots if possible. Do not attempt to vote again — we will confirm next steps if an issue is validated.
