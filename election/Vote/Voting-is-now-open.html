Your chance to choose the next KPA officer team — voting is open from 9:00 am Tue 28 Oct to 5:00 pm Thu 30 Oct 2025.

Dear Keele Postgraduate,

Voting for the KPA By-Election is now live. You will shortly receive a **separate email** containing your unique voting link (this is sent to your Keele email address). Please follow that link to access your personalised voting card and cast your vote.

Key dates & times

Voting opens: Tue 28 Oct 2025 — 9:00 am
Voting closes: Thu 30 Oct 2025 — 5:00 pm
Results announced: Thu 30 Oct 2025 — 7:00 pm (Clubhouse & online)

How to vote
  • Open the email with the subject line from the voting system (contains your unique link).
  • Click the link and rank candidates in order of preference for each role (this election uses the Single Transferable Vote method).
  • Submit before the deadline — you may change your choices any time before polls close.

What if you don’t get a voting email? — Check your Junk/Spam folder first. If it’s not there, contact us immediately at kpa.elections@keele.ac.uk or reply to this message and we’ll help.

Read our step-by-step voting guide here: LINK_TO_VOTING_GUIDE. The guide explains STV and shows screenshots of the voting card.
