KPA By-Election Information Session
Thinking of standing or just want to find out more? Join us in person or online to learn everything about nominations, campaigning and voting.

Dear Keele Postgraduate,

You are invited to the KPA By-Election information session. This session is open to all postgraduate students — whether you’re thinking about standing, recommending a friend, or just want to understand how the election works.

Event details

📅 Date: Fri 3 Oct 2025
🕔 Time: 5:30 pm – 7:00 pm
📍 Location: Room CBA1.102 & Online via Teams

What we’ll cover:

  • Available voluntary officer roles and what each role involves.
  • How to nominate yourself and the nomination deadlines.
  • Campaign rules, permitted materials and where you can campaign.
  • Hustings, voting (how the e-vote works) and appeals process.
  • Where to get help and answers to any questions you have.
Read the By-Election Handbook
All postgraduates are welcome. If you cannot attend, the session will be recorded and uploaded to the KPA website shortly after the event.
