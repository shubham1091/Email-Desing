Attach your files to an email (or include a download link) and send to kpa.elections@keele.ac.uk with the subject line: Campaign materials — [Your full name] — [Role]. Use these file names if possible: Lastname_Firstname_Manifesto.pdf, Lastname_Firstname_Poster.jpg.

If you anticipate being late because of technical issues (large file sizes, upload problems), email us immediately at kpa.elections@keele.ac.uk with a link to the files and a brief explanation — do not assume an extension will be granted. We will only accept late corrections at the Returning Officer’s discretion.
