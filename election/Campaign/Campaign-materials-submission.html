Keele Postgraduate Association • By-Elections (Oct–Nov 2025)
Campaign materials — action required
Submit your campaign materials
Deadline: Wednesday 22 Oct 2025, 12:00 pm. Please send your manifesto, poster and optional video to the elections team now.

Dear Candidate,

This is a reminder to submit your campaign materials for the KPA By-Election. All materials must be received by 12:00 pm on Wednesday 22 October 2025. Failure to submit a manifesto by the deadline will result in automatic disqualification (see the Election Handbook).

What to submit & format
  • Manifesto: PDF or high-quality JPEG. Maximum: 2 sides of A4 (keep within the two-side limit).
  • Poster (optional): JPEG or PDF — single A4 page. This may be used for online and window printing.
  • Campaign video (optional): MP4 — max 1 minute.
File naming & sending

Please name your files as follows before sending:

  • Manifesto: Lastname_Firstname_Manifesto.pdf
  • Poster: Lastname_Firstname_Poster.jpg
  • Video: Lastname_Firstname_Video.mp4

Send all files in one email to: kpa.elections@keele.ac.uk with the subject line: Campaign materials — [Your full name] — [Role].

Printing allocation & approvals
  • Each candidate is allocated 30 printed A4 pages (specify how you will use them — A4/A5, posters vs manifesto).
  • The Returning Officer team will review materials and confirm approval by Thursday 23 Oct, 12:00 pm. Approved materials will be printed the same day.
Important rules & consequences
  • Do not include any copyrighted logos or trademarked material (including KPA/KeeleSU logos) unless explicitly permitted.
  • If your materials breach the rules, we will request edits — failure to supply corrected materials before the printing deadline may lead to disqualification.
  • Only approved materials may be used during campaigning (online or in-person).
Send materials to kpa.elections@keele.ac.uk
If you have large files (video or high-res images) and need to use WeTransfer/Drive, please email the Returning Officer in advance so we can confirm receipt and download.
