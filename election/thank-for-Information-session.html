Dear Keele Postgraduate,

Thank you to everyone who attended the KPA By-Election information session. We hope it answered your questions and helped you decide whether to stand or get involved. Below are quick links and next steps.

What we covered
  • Available voluntary officer roles and what each role involves.
  • How to nominate yourself and where to find nomination forms and deadlines.
  • Campaign rules, permitted materials and how the review & printing works.
  • Hustings format, voting (how the e-vote / STV works) and the appeals process.
  • Where to get help and who to contact with questions.

If you were unable to attend and have questions after watching the recording, or if you'd like to discuss standing for a role, please email the Returning Officer team at kpa.elections@keele.ac.uk. We’re happy to help and can arrange one-to-one calls if needed.

Reminder: full election details, timelines and nomination instructions are in the handbook and on the KPA website — if you plan to stand, please read the handbook carefully.
