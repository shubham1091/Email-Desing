Keele Postgraduate Association • By-Elections (Oct–Nov 2025)
Candidate Briefing — Attendance Requirement
Disqualification Notice — Non-Attendance at Candidate Briefing
Issued in accordance with the KPA By-Election Rules, October 2025

Dear Candidate,

We regret to inform you that, in line with the KPA By-Election Rules (Appendix 1, Section 2.1) set out in the By-Election Handbook 2025–26, you have been disqualified from the current KPA By-Election process.

Attendance at the mandatory Candidate Briefing held on Thursday 16 October 2025 at 6:00 pm was a compulsory requirement for all candidates. This session ensures that every candidate fully understands the election rules, campaign requirements, and key deadlines.

As you did not attend and did not provide a valid reason or request an alternative arrangement in advance, your candidacy has been withdrawn in accordance with the official election regulations. This decision has been made under the authority of the Returning Officers and is consistent with the KPA Election Handbook.

Appeals Procedure
If you believe this decision was made in error or wish to appeal due to exceptional circumstances, you may submit a written appeal to kpa.appeals@keele.ac.uk within 72 hours of this notice.

Appeals must clearly state your grounds and include supporting evidence, as outlined in Section 6 of the By-Election Handbook.

Thank you for your understanding and for your interest in standing for election. We encourage you to stay engaged with KPA activities and future opportunities to represent the postgraduate community.
