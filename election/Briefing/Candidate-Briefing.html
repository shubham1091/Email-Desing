Candidate Briefing — Thursday 16 October, 6:00 pm
This briefing is compulsory for all candidates — important rules, deadlines and campaign guidance will be covered.

Dear Candidate,

Thank you for nominating yourself in the KPA By-Election. The Returning Officer team will host a **mandatory Candidate Briefing** to walk through the election process, rules and next steps. All candidates **must** attend — failure to attend without a valid reason and alternative arrangements may result in disqualification.

Candidate Briefing — details

📅 Date: Thursday 16 Oct 2025
🕕 Time: 6:00 pm (please arrive 10 minutes early)
📍 Location: CBA1.102 & Online via Teams

Agenda highlights:

  • Election rules, permitted campaign materials and submission process.
  • Manifesto & poster format, printing allocations and deadlines.
  • Hustings format, timings and expectations (2 minute speech + Q&A).
  • Voting process and how votes are cast and counted.
  • Conduct, appeals procedure and disqualification grounds.
  • Q&A — bring any questions you have about campaigning or rules.
What to bring / prepare
  • Your Keele student email address and student number.
  • A draft of your manifesto or poster if you want quick feedback (optional).
  • Any queries about accessibility or reasonable adjustments.
