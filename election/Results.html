Keele Postgraduate Association • By-Elections (Oct–Nov 2025)
Election results — officially announced
The results are in 🗳
Thank you to everyone who voted in this year’s KPA By-Election. We’re delighted to share the final results and congratulate our newly elected officers!

Dear Postgraduate Member,

The Returning Officer team is pleased to announce the results of the KPA By-Election 2025. Thank you to all candidates for their dedication and to every postgraduate who participated.

🎉 Elected Officers (Term: 3 Nov 2025 – 30 Jun 2026)
Activities Officer: John Ucheagwu
International Officer: Vasudha Duvvuru Madhusudhanan

A total of 223 valid ballots were cast out of 4,980 registered voters, representing a 4% turnout — a strong show of engagement for a by-election. Both winning candidates received majority mandates under the Single Transferable Vote (STV) system.

Appeals Process

Members wishing to lodge an appeal regarding the conduct of the election or result may email kpa.appeals@keele.ac.uk within 72 hours of this announcement, in line with the KPA Election Handbook.

Next Steps
  • New officers will begin their term on Monday, 3rd November 2025.
  • Official handover and induction sessions will be scheduled in the coming week.
  • Recordings of the Hustings and Results event will be uploaded to the KPA website.
