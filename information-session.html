Available Voluntary Positions

Voluntary roles are development opportunities — you’ll receive support and training. Term runs from 3 Nov 2025 to 30 Jun 2026.

International Officer

Champion international students, improve transition and cultural events, represent international PGs in committees.

Equality & Diversity Officer

Promote accessibility and inclusion at KPA events, run at least one E&D campaign with ring-fenced budget.

Activities Officer

Plan events and socials for postgraduates, coordinate with SU and clubhouse, manage event volunteers and budgets.

Why stand?

  • • Leadership and project management experience for your CV.
  • • Direct impact on student experience and services.
  • • Networking with university staff and external partners.

International Officer — Deliverables

  • • One arrival/transition campaign and at least one international culture week.
  • • Written plan for outreach to international students and accessible event formats.
  • • Monthly report to the KPA Exec on international engagement metrics.

Equality & Diversity Officer — Deliverables

  • • A targeted E&D campaign (specific measurable objective + budget plan).
  • • Accessibility audit recommendations for KPA events and communications.
  • • Representation at relevant university working groups.

Activities Officer — Deliverables

  • • Run a termly events calendar with budgets and volunteer plans.
  • • Post-event reports (attendance, feedback, costs) for Exec review.
  • • Partnership proposals with SU/Clubhouse for sustained activities.

Nomination Process — Step-by-step

Eligibility

  • • Registered Keele postgraduate at close of voting.
  • • Minimum 4 months registration remaining after voting close.
  • • Nominations must be from your Keele student email account.

How to nominate (email)

  1. 1. Compose an email from your Keele account to kpa.elections@keele.ac.uk
  2. 2. Include: full name, student number and role you are standing for
  3. 3. Send before 5pm, Wed 15th Oct

Materials & Deadlines

  • • Manifesto — max 2 A4 (PDF/JPEG) — content must be original and not copyrighted.
  • • Poster — 1 A4 (JPEG)
  • • Video — max 1 minute (MP4)
  • • All materials to be submitted by 12pm, Weds 22nd Oct for approval.
  • • KPA will print up to 30 A4 pages per candidate (specify A4/A5 mix when submitting).

Election Rules — Conduct & Materials

Conduct

  • • Positive campaigning only — focus on policies, not personalities.
  • • No harassment, bribery, incentives, or targeted pressure in private spaces.
  • • No staff, trustees or SU officers may endorse candidates publicly in their official capacity.
  • • Breaches may result in investigation and disqualification by the CRO.

Materials

  • • Manifesto (max 2 A4), Poster (1 A4), Video (≤1 minute).
  • • No copyrighted logos (KPA/University/etc.) or third-party trademarks.
  • • Submit in JPEG/MP4/PDF formats by 12pm, 22 Oct for review.
  • • KPA provides up to 30 printed pages per candidate; candidates cannot produce additional printed materials.

Campaigning logistics

The RO team will post candidate materials to the KPA website and social channels once approved. Candidates may approach voters in communal campus spaces but must not campaign in teaching rooms, library study areas, private accommodation, or closed groups.

Voting & Appeals

Voting

Voting opens at 9am on Tue 28 Oct and closes at 5pm on Thu 30 Oct. Voting is electronic and uses the Single Transferable Vote (STV) method. Each registered postgraduate receives a unique voting link via email.

Appeals

Appeals must be submitted in writing to kpa.appeals@keele.ac.uk within 72 hours of results. Grounds: clear procedural error, RO bias, or evidence of a successful candidate breaking rules.

Results publication

Results will be announced at the Clubhouse (7pm, Thu 30 Oct) and published on the KPA website with a breakdown of rounds where relevant. The RO will notify senior stakeholders as required.

Next Steps & Q&A Summary

For prospective candidates

  1. • Draft manifesto early — keep to two A4 pages and proofread.
  2. • Submit materials well before 22 Oct to allow time for edits.
  3. • Prepare a 2-minute hustings speech and short answers to likely questions.

For supporters & volunteers

  • • Do not create or run paid adverts or private mailing lists for candidates.
  • • Ensure any volunteering is transparent and follows the candidate rules.
  • • Report concerns to kpa.elections@keele.ac.uk — do not engage in public disputes.
